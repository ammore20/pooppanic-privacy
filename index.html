Privacy Policy for Poop Panic: Save the Toilet

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game “Poop Panic: Save the Toilet” (the “app”), published by Buzzuka (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

The app saves your best score on your device only. This stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising and third parties

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising service described above.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You can also stop all data use by the app by uninstalling it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected by Google AdMob is managed by Google; see the link above for your controls.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with a new effective date.
